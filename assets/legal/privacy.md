Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

LapTime ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the LapTime mobile application.

Information We Collect

Account Information
When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and authentication details through Apple Sign-In, Google Sign-In, or email/password registration.

Session Data
When you record a track session, we collect GPS location data, device sensor data (accelerometer, gyroscope, barometer, magnetometer), and weather information for the session location.

Device Information
We may collect device type, operating system version, and app version for crash reporting and analytics purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide and improve the LapTime service
- Record and display your lap times and telemetry data
- Enable social features such as following other drivers and team functionality
- Generate leaderboards and performance analytics
- Diagnose technical issues and improve app performance

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data in the following circumstances:
- Public sessions: If you mark a session as public, other users can see your lap times, circuit, and car information
- Teams: Team members can view each other's sessions
- Service providers: We use Supabase for data storage and authentication
- Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL) with row-level security policies. Session data is stored locally on your device and synced to our servers when connected.

Your Rights

You can:
- View and export your data through the app
- Delete your account and associated data
- Control session privacy (public/private)
- Manage who can see your sessions

GPS and Sensor Data

GPS and sensor data is collected only during active recording sessions. This data is used exclusively for lap timing, telemetry analysis, and circuit identification. We do not track your location outside of recording sessions.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy within the app.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@laptime.app.
